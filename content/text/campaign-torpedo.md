The words of the torpedo boats (card space-game-torpedo-boats): what the ship's radio says when a boat arms its tube, when a torpedo is away, when one is shot down or hits, the names of the two boat kinds in the map's list, and the map's legend rows for a boat and a torpedo. They live in their own file because content/text/ui.md is at its row limit; the same lines are the defaults in src/contenttables.js (the test contentui holds the two together). The lines are curt, and the pirates' boat is never called an alien.
