The words of the new keys page (card space-game-screens-e-keys-settings; Troy, 25 Nov 2026; the mock is docs/screens-mock.html, section Keys page): three groups, one chip and two or three words a row. The chips are never words: they are read from the key table (src/data_controls.js CAMP_KEYS) by src/scrkeys.js. Shown only, never said.
