The words of the Campaign's friendly outposts (card space-game-campaign-outposts): the refusals of docking, building and repairing away from an outpost, the retreat line, the Fabricator, and the dock's ready room. They are strings of the game like those of ui.md, kept in a file of their own because ui.md is at the 200-row limit of a content file.
