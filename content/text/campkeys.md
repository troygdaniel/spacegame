The words of the Campaign's page of every key and of its links (card space-game-campaign-keys-help): the page's title, its footer and the heading of the debug keys, and the line on the title screen's Campaign panel. The keys themselves and what they do are the CAMPAIGN group of src/data_controls.js. They are strings of the game like those of ui.md and are kept in a file of their own because ui.md is at its row limit.

## Strings
| key | text |
|---|---|
| camp.keys.title | CAMPAIGN  -  ALL THE KEYS |
| camp.keys.footer | H or Esc closes this page. The battle waits while it is open. |
| camp.keys.debug | DEBUG KEYS (THE TUNE KIT) |
| title.camp.keys | H  all the keys |
| camp.sel.all | All pilots |
| camp.ui.pausepanels | {keywlast} {keywnext} pick who, {keylast} {keynext} an enemy, {keyflast} {keyfnext} a friendly, {keyattack} engage, {keyguard} guard, {keyrecall} recall. {cruiserkey} the cruiser: fly, mine, dock, shields. In a list: Up, Down, Enter. {keyfocus} looks at it. H lists every key. |
| camp.keys.roomtitle | READY ROOM  -  ALL THE KEYS |
| camp.keys.roomfooter | H or Esc closes this page. |
