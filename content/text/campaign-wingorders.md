The words of the Campaign's wing orders with groups and explicit targets (card space-game-campaign-wing-targets): the answers to an order that names a group and a target ("Fighters, attack the mothership."), and the plain refusals ("Fighters cannot escort an enemy."). They live in their own file because content/text/ui.md is at its row limit; the same lines are the defaults in src/contenttables.js (the test contentui holds the two together).
