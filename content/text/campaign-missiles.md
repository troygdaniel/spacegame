The words of the missiles' ore (card space-game-missiles-cost-ore, src/campmissilebuild.js): a missile costs ore to build, the ship builds one by itself at 0, R builds the rack up. camp.cruiser.* are the two words in the status block at the top left (src/campcruiserhud.js); camp.say.* and camp.no.* are the order's answers (the radio, the toast and the S list's reasons).

## Strings
| key | text |
|---|---|
| camp.cruiser.missiles | MISSILES |
| camp.cruiser.building | building {s} s |
| camp.cruiser.noore | no ore |
| camp.say.reload | Reloading: {n} more, {ore} ore. |
| camp.say.rackdry | Out of ore, pilot. The rack stops at {n}. |
| camp.no.rackfull | The rack is full, pilot. |
| camp.no.reloading | Already building the rack, pilot. |
