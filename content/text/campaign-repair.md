The words of the Campaign's fighter repair (card space-game-fighter-repair): a fighter under 30 percent hull breaks off, lands on the cruiser, is mended for about 10 seconds and goes out again. The ship's voice says it each way (camp.say.*: a toast and a spoken line, curt, about 12 words) and the HUD's wing strip shows the fighters being mended (camp.ui.repair). Strings of the game like those of ui.md, in a file of their own because ui.md is at its row limit.

## Strings
| key | text |
|---|---|
| camp.say.repairoff | Fighter hit hard. Breaking off to land. |
| camp.say.repairback | Fighter mended and launching again. |
| camp.say.repairdone | Fighter mended. It is in the bay. |
| camp.ui.repair | REPAIR {n}  {s} s |
