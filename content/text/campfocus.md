The words of the focus view (card space-game-focus-view): the label that shows while the camera watches a ship (what is watched and the key that comes back), the name of the cruiser as a thing to watch, the arrow at the edge of the screen that points at the cruiser with its distance, and the toast when the watched ship is gone. The keys are read from the key table.

## Strings
| key | text |
|---|---|
| camp.hud.focuswatch | WATCHING {name}  {keyzoomreset} back |
| camp.hud.focuscruiser | the cruiser |
| camp.hud.focuscruiserfar | Cruiser {km} km |
| camp.hud.focuslost | {name} is gone. Back to the cruiser. |
