The words of command time (card space-game-command-slowtime): the sign that is up while a panel, the map or a call is open and the battle runs slowly, and the line that says why the first time. A key may be in any file of this folder.

## Strings
| key | text |
|---|---|
| camp.slow.sign | COMMAND TIME  x{scale} |
| camp.slow.hint | Time slows while you give orders. |
