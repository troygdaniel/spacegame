The words of the convoy orders (card space-game-convoy-orders): what the ship says when the pilot tells the escorted ships (the transport, the haulers, the neutral miners) to tuck in under the cruiser's shield, hold, run for it or carry on, and why an order cannot be given. {who} is the one ship's name, or "Convoy" for all of them. They live in their own file because content/text/ui.md is at its row limit; the same lines are the defaults in src/contenttables.js (the test contentui holds the two together).

## Strings
| key | text |
|---|---|
| camp.say.ctuck | {who}, tuck in under our shield. |
| camp.say.chold | {who}, hold here. |
| camp.say.crun | {who}, run for it. No cover. |
| camp.say.ccarry | {who}, carry on. |
| camp.say.cwho | Convoy |
| camp.no.cnone | No escorted ships to command. |
| camp.no.cwhat | Tuck in, hold here, run for it, or carry on? |
