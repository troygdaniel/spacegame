The words of the Campaign's radio chatter (card space-game-campaign-chatter). The wing's pilots call a kill, a hit, an order, a wave, the story: one short fixed line each, about 6 words, with no number and no {placeholder} inside, so each line can be recorded once. A key is camp.chat.<call>.<n>: add a line by giving the next n. They live in a file of their own because content/text/ui.md reached the 200 rows a file may have.
