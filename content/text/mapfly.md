The words of the map's click-to-fly buttons (card space-game-map-click-fly): the Campaign map's two small buttons by a place you have set, and the line the autopilot says when it flies fast. They are strings of the game like those of ui.md and are kept here, in a file of their own, so ui.md stays under the 200-row limit and the change can be undone alone.

## Strings
| key | text |
|---|---|
| camp.map.fly | FLY    Enter          {t} |
| camp.map.fast | FAST   Shift+Enter    {t} |
| camp.map.set | SET: {name} |
| camp.nav.gofast | Autopilot to {name}, full burn. Any steering key takes the wheel. |
