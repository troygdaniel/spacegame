| title.long.sub | The trading run to Kepler-4b. Enter starts it; the arrows pick. |
| title.camp.key | K |
| title.camp.name | CAMPAIGN |
| title.camp.sub | Earth's fleet on the rim: fly the cruiser, command the wing. |
