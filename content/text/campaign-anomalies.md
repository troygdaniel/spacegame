The words of the Campaign's anomalies (card space-game-campaign-anomalies): the cloaked enemy caches, what the ship says when it notices one, when the cloak drops and when the cache is looted, what a reward is called, and the two techs looted from them. They live in their own file because content/text/ui.md is at its row limit; the same lines are the defaults in src/contenttables.js (the test contentui holds the two together).
