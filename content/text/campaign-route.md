The words of the fork in an escort's road (card space-game-scout-route): what the ship says when the road forks, when the convoy waits for a lane, when a scout has read one and when the pick is refused, and the button of the Tab map. Lines the ship speaks are about 12 words at most.

| key | text |
|---|---|

| camp.say.lanepick | Taking the {lane}. |
| camp.say.laneauto | No word. Taking the {lane}. |
| camp.say.lanefound | Scout {n}, {name}: {what}. |
| camp.no.lanewhat | Which lane, pilot? |
| camp.no.lanelocked | Too late, pilot. We are on the {lane}. |
