The words of the big briefing in the ready room (card space-game-campaign-briefing): its headings, the enemies, the readiness and its warnings, the route, and the WALKTHROUGH of each mission, a few curt lines (about twelve words, no spoilers) read by key camp.bf.<level>.<mission>.w<n>. The walkthrough lines are fixed lines: the voice pack records them. A mission with none shows a plain line.
