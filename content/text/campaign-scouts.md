The words of the Campaign's scout-escorts (card space-game-campaign-scouts): the Scout order's answers (camp.say.*, camp.no.*: spoken in the ship's voice and shown as a toast), a scout's reports, the HUD count and the Tab map's third button. In a file of their own because content/text/ui.md reached the 200 rows a file may have. {n} is the scout's number, {name} its place, {likely} what an anomaly is likely to be.
