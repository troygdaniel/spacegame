The words of the Campaign's ship systems (card space-game-campaign-systems): power to weapons, shields and engines, six pips to share. The lines the orders answer with (camp.say.*, camp.no.*, spoken in the ship's voice and shown as a toast), the two the computer says when the shields or the engines run low, and the three names the HUD uses. They live in a file of their own because content/text/ui.md reached the 200 rows a file may have.
