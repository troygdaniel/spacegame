The words of the Campaign's navigation: the autopilot, the list on the map, the arrow at the screen edge (card space-game-campaign-navigate). They are strings of the game like those of ui.md and are kept in a file of their own because ui.md is at the 200-row limit of a content file.

## Strings

|---|---|
| camp.nav.go | Autopilot to {name}. Any steering key takes the wheel. |
| camp.nav.off | Autopilot off. You have the wheel. |
| camp.nav.cleared | Destination cleared. |
| camp.nav.arrived | {name}: arrived. |
| camp.nav.lost | Autopilot off: {name} is gone. |
