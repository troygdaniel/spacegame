The words of the night hunt's small fixes on the Campaign (card space-game-night-hunt-l2-l3): a convoy that is a ship with a name (the rim run's Transport and Hauler 1 to 3) arrives and is lost as a ship, not as "the convoy to <place>". A key may be in any file of this folder.

| camp.say.shiplost | {name} is lost. |

| camp.o.deliver.transport | Transport arrived: {got} of {need}. |
| camp.lose.convoy.transport | The transport is lost. |
