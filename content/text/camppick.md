The words of the target pick (card space-game-order-target-pick; Troy, 22 Nov 2026): an order that needs a target takes the one candidate at once, or opens a sub-list of the several (Mine ore: a field of rocks; Repair a ship we protect: a hurt ship). The sub-list's heading by what is picked, the foot's keys, a rock field's name and count, the line of a candidate, the refusal when there is nothing to attack, and the foot of a list a part's row opened (Esc goes back). The rows' own words are in the key table (src/data_controls.js).
