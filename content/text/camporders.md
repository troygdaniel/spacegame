The words of the pilots' calls for orders (card space-game-order-calls, src/camporders.js, src/camporderdraw.js). A call is one fixed radio line (no number, no name, so the voice pack records it once) and three choices on 1 2 3; each choice has a label on the prompt and an answer the pilot says back; no answer, and the pilot says the late line. The calls are on every level, with pirates and with aliens, so no line says which they are. A key may be in any file of this folder.
