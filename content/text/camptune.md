The words of the Campaign's tuning panel (card space-game-tuning-panel; Troy, 22 Nov 2026): the N key opens a list of numbers and switches that are changed live, kept in the browser, and copied out for an agent to bake in as the defaults. The heading, its notes and key lines, the two top rows, a group's heading, each row's name (camp.ui.tune.row.<id>, one per row of CAMP_TUNE in src/camptune.js), when a change is felt, and the TUNED mark on the screen. The key is a placeholder from the key table (src/data_controls.js).
