The words of the Fill the window switch (card space-game-fill-window-a-flight; Troy, 25 Nov 2026; src/fillwindow.js): a button of Settings > CONTENT, beside the New screens row, and the line Settings says when it is pressed. With it on, the Campaign's flight takes the window's shape (no dark bands; the view shows more space along the window's long side, nothing is stretched or cut); with it off the game keeps its 16:9 shape. Kept in the browser; ?fill=1 and ?fill=0 in the page's address are the tests' way. None of these is spoken.

## Strings
| key | text |
|---|---|
| fill.set.on | Fill the window: ON |
| fill.set.off | Fill the window: OFF |
| fill.set.said.on | Fill the window ON: the flight takes the window's shape. |
| fill.set.said.off | Fill the window OFF: the game keeps its 16:9 shape. |
