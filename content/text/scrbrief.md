The words of the one-page briefing in the new look (card space-game-screens-b-briefing, piece b of the new screens; the mock is docs/screens-mock.html): the heading, the bonus label, a mission's four moments (scr.bf.<level>.<mission>.m1 to m4, two or three words each) and the goal line a mission's briefing says (scr.m.<level>.<mission>.goal: the ship's ONE spoken sentence, voiced; where a mission has no line of its own the briefing says its camp.m.<level>.<mission>.b1).

| scr.bf.headreplay | Replay · mission {n} · {kind} |
| scr.bf.bonus | Bonus |
| scr.bf.1.1.m1 | Patrol leaves |

| scr.bf.1.1.m4 | Pirate leader |
