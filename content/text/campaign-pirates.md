The words of the pirates and the neutral traffic of Level 1 (card space-game-l1-pirates-traffic): what the ship says when a pirate wave breaks off or one runs, when a patrol or a miner is lost, the names of the new kinds in the map's list, and the map's legend rows. They live in their own file because content/text/ui.md is at its row limit; the same lines are the defaults in src/contenttables.js (the test contentui holds the two together).
