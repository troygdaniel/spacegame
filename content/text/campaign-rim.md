The words of Level 1 as the rim run (card space-game-l1-rim-missions): what the ship says in the three missions, the objective lines and the bonus for the ore, the sale at the Works, the debrief line for lost haulers. Lines the ship speaks are about 12 words at most. The story lines and the briefing lines of the three missions replace the old ones in their own files (campaign-belt.md, campaign-missions.md, campbrief.md, ui.md).
