The freighter MULE leaves the hangar when you do,
bound for the Perseus gate to the south-west.
Pirates know the route. Fly with her and shoot
anything that gets close. She pays on arrival.
Lose her and you get nothing.

## Board line
active: Escort the MULE to the Perseus gate
failed: Failed. The MULE was lost

## Progress
The MULE is under way to the Perseus gate.
Stay with her. Do not dock or land.

## Steps
- Take the escort call
- Launch with the MULE
- Escort the MULE to the gate

## Failure
Ship destroyed. The MULE is on her own. Mission failed.
