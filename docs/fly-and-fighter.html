Fly and fighter

What the built game measures on main 4c4f584, mission by mission, in the cruiser and in a fighter. Then how to make the fighter a game of its own, and how to teach the first five minutes without a wall of text. No key is moved anywhere on this page.

Mission by mission, measured

Measured with the repo's bots, three seeds a mission, steady skill: plain flies the cruiser; ace is plain plus a player flying a pilot's fighter; sit takes the stick and does nothing. "Taken" is damage to the cruiser. The last two columns are judged.

The fighter's game: how hostile fighters fly

Today a small hostile turns onto its target and flies at it. In a fighter that makes every fight a head-on pass you win by holding Space. Three moves, all for the ship kinds that exist, give you something to chase and something to shake off.

The fighter's game: being shot down

The fighter bot was never shot down in any mission, so today this cannot be seen. Proposed: it is a setback you feel for twenty seconds, not the end of the mission.

The first five minutes: a key row that teaches

Today the bottom row lists five keys from the first second and never changes. C is not on it, nor on the keys page. Proposed: the same row, the same keys, but a chip lights once at the moment its key first matters, with two or three words beside it, and goes quiet again when you press it.
