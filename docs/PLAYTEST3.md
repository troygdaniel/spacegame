## Checked and fine

- **Mods**: O opens the Mods screen, 1 turns the example pack on, the status says "Something changed. Restart to apply it." and the restart button lights; Esc closes it without opening Pause; after a reload the Title says MODDED and the rescue pays 650. A hostile picked pack (wrong types, a 9000-character line, a system with a negative size, junk in the on-list) is refused field by field: 11 problems, "CONTENT: 11 PROBLEMS | MODDED" on the Title, the game starts and plays with the example pack's rescue at 650.
- **Saves**: a truncated save is a new game (credits 0, one Wren, a fresh 561-character save written). A save of wrong ids (system "nowhere", dock "no-such-station", active 7, model "zzz", hull 9999, fuel -5, engine tier 9, mission "rescued" with id "nope") wakes at Halcyon with the Wren, active 0, parts clamped (engine 3), fuel 0, mission none, credits kept, and (after the fix) the Bastion parked at Halcyon too. A full trip saves and reloads byte for byte, with the renamed Kite flying and the Wren parked.
- **A save made in Kestrel space wakes you at Halcyon** (`system` kestrel, `lastDock` halcyon-station in the file; `system` halcyon after the reload). That is the design (`docs/UNIVERSE.md` 8.2) and it costs the player nothing; it also means a reload is a free ride home with the fuel already spent. Worth a line in the design if Troy minds.
- **Pump**: "The tank is already full." on 1 with a full tank; "You have no credits for fuel." at 0 cr; with 100 cr and 35 units of room it fills 12.5 units and says "Partly filled"; with 500 cr it fills the rest for 180 cr. Esc closes it and Pause stays shut.

## Economy sanity

Tier 1 parts cost 250 to 400 on the Wren (200 to 320 on the Kite, 450 to 720 on the Bastion); the rescue pays 500; a round trip to Kestrel burns 7.6 units in the Wren, 61 cr at the Halcyon pump. A new player affords the first part with the first rescue, about ten minutes in, as `docs/ECONOMY.md` says. The Kite at 4,500 is nine rescues.
