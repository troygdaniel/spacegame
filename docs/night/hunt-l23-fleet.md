# Hunt: Levels 2 and 3 after the fleet command batch (card `space-game-hunt-l23-fleet`, 1-GEN, 21 Nov 2026)

Main `79e9f14`. The six missions of levels 2 and 3 were tuned by bots after auto shields, fighter repair, pilot calls, command time, the gauges and laser charge, but nobody had flown them by hand. Bug fixes only (feature freeze); anything that is tuning goes to 2-GEN and anything that is a decision goes to Troy, neither is in code. 7-GEN is remapping every key (card `space-game-two-hands-keys`): nothing here touches key handling, hints or boot scripts, and the walks read the panel keys from the key table (`CAMP_PANELS`), never from a letter written in them.

**How it was hunted.**
1. *The sim, a tape per mission*: the plain bot flies each mission and every second of it is written down (hull, shield, laser charge, power, ore, the wing's hull and `R` for a fighter being mended, every call and its answer, every radio line, every repair event).
2. *The real game, a walk per mission* (muted, one Chrome through the test lock, parts under five minutes each): K, the ready room, the launch, the story, the burn to the mission's place with the SHIP panel's goal row and Shift, the wing out with the FLEET panel's launch row, then the cruiser steered with held W A D and Space and the panels, the calls (1 2 3) and the emergency row pressed as a player would. Flown with calls answered well, answered badly and not answered. Then the debrief, the pick or the end, the ready room and its overlays (missions, refit, rank), a looted cache (decloak, guards, reward), the Level 3 repair aboard, and quitting at the pick.
3. *A fuzz in the sim*: the six missions, 6 seeds each, with and without command time, a random order of every kind pressed every second or two (every order of `campDo`, the calls, power, emergency, missiles, stance, convoy orders, build, dock). After every step the numbers are checked: finite, shield and charge in range, no negative store or bay, no fractional bay, no ship twice, no fighter more than there were, a call never waiting at full speed. 72 runs: no violation (two 3.3 runs were still going at 200 s: the fuzz's random orders slowed them, not a fault).
4. Pictures to `~/dev/spacegame-night/shots/h23-*.png` (not in the repository).
