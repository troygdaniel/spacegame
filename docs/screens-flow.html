Screens flow

The screens around the flying: the walk, the problems, the steps. Written for PROMPT 21 (BRIEF 12), 27 Nov 2026, from the live build at main ed51f8f, walked in headless Chrome at 1600 by 900 with the real keys. The flight screen itself is out of scope. Word counts are every word, number and key cap on the screen.

In one paragraph. The look is right and most screens are already short. The cost to the player is in three places. One key shows a blank screen (H on the title). Trying again after a loss puts one or two old-look cards and up to 95 words back in the way. And the fastest way to fly is not the one the lit key leads to: following Enter takes four presses and passes the mission grid, while L L takes three and nobody is told. Seven small steps are built in this PR; nine choices are left for Troy.

1. The map

Every screen, every key that moves between them. Pink marks a dead end or a surprise found on the walk; the number is the problem in section 3.

Not drawn: H opens a keys page from the ready room, grid, briefing, refit, pause and flight, and H or Esc closes it back to where it was. S opens Settings from the title and the pause card. In the briefing Left Right walk the map, M changes the map, - = Q zoom. Esc from a briefing goes back to where it was opened (ready room or grid).

Voiced lines

No voiced line's words are changed. The words of these screens are shown, never spoken (the one spoken family, the mission's goal line the ship says when a briefing opens, is untouched). Step 2 changes when lines are heard, not their words: on a retry the level's story lines are no longer read out again with the story card. There is no voiced line I would ask to change.

What I was unsure of

  • Step 2 and the old look. With ?screens=0 (the tests' switch) a retry still shows the old cards, as before; only the new look skips them. A retry started from the address bar is untouched.
  • Step 4 adds one screen to the Esc role's list in src/data_controls.js (the lost card). No key is moved.
  • Step 6's page title. With one group on the page, "READY ROOM - ALL THE KEYS" would be untrue, so the one-group page uses the flight page's title, "Keys". The old title key is left in place for the old look.
  • Lost-card word counts were read with the debug lose key, which has no tip line; a real loss adds a tip of about 20 words.
  • The walk was at one window size. Fill-the-window was not changed by any step.
