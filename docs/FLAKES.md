# Flaky tests and runs

What fails on a busy or sleeping Mac, how often, why, and what was done. Written on 14 Nov 2026 from the 61 full smoke runs found in the test logs on one Mac (11 to 14 Nov: 40 passed, 21 failed), DreamBot's notes on the first batch gate run, and the sightings of the people running tests. With seven agents the load average passed 80 on 10 cores; the smoke is one Chrome at a time (`tests/lock.mjs`), and its slowest part is waiting its turn (DreamBot's first batch: 202 "waiting for another Long Haul test run" lines, about 3.6 hours for 38 steps), not the tests.

**Before you call something a flake:** run that test alone (`node tests/<name>.mjs`). If it passes alone and the failure says "timed out", "DevTools call timed out", "waiting for the test lock" or "Chrome did not start", it is load or sleep and is here. If it fails alone, it is a real failure on your branch. The list of failures that are not flakes is at the end.

| Test or step | Symptom | How often | Cause | Fix or workaround |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The test lock (`tests/lock.mjs`), any Chrome test | `timed out after 2700 s waiting for the test lock held by pid N` | 2 logs | Two causes. (1) Waiters polled at random, so one run (`tests/perfart.mjs`) waited the 45 minutes while later runs passed it. (2) The wait was timed with the wall clock: when the Mac slept for about an hour in the middle of DreamBot's step 4g (`shipmode.mjs`) the wait was over on waking, and every holder's heartbeat looked an hour old until its next beat, so a live run's lock could have been taken. | **Fixed (1)** by the arrival-order queue (`1b38207`). **Fixed (2):** the wait is timed on the monotonic clock (`performance.now()`, which does not tick asleep); when a waiting run sees the wall clock jump away from it, it takes no lock or ticket as stale from its file time for about 35 s. `tests/locktest.mjs` jumps the clock an hour: the wait still lasts its time and a live holder keeps its lock; its control (a clock that ticks asleep) ends the wait in 0.4 s, as before. |
| Any Chrome test | `DevTools call timed out: Runtime.evaluate (after 120 s)`, or `Page.captureScreenshot` | 16 messages in 14 of the 492 test logs on this Mac (several are full smoke runs). 12 of the 14 were on 12 and 13 Nov, most of them before the one-Chrome lock landed on main (13 Nov 19:07; branches cut earlier did not have it); the mission flow test (`missions.mjs`, the longest) is the one most often hit (3 failed smoke runs); 2 on 14 Nov at 16:12 and 16:18 with load over 80. | The page is starved: Chrome's renderer is not scheduled for 120 s while many other Chrome runs, builds and test suites share the CPU. The call limit was a flat 120 s. | **The lock removed most of them. Now also:** `patience()` in `tests/cdp.mjs` stretches the call limit and every `waitFor` limit by the load per core (never under 1 times, at most 4 times; an idle Mac gets the limits it always had). DreamBot's gate retries a load-only timeout up to three times at low load; not duplicated in `smoke.sh`. |
| `shipmode.mjs` "timed out waiting for: back at the station", `missions.mjs` after death | `timed out waiting for: back at the station` | 2 failed smoke runs | An 8 s real-time wait for a scene to start (a fade, then the scene's art) under load, timed on the wall clock. | **Fixed** with `patience()` and the monotonic clock in `waitFor`. A wait for something that never happens still ends (up to 4 times later under load). |
| `tests/fuel.mjs` (and any test): the process stays after its last line | `FUEL PASS (71 checks)` printed, then nothing: the node process and its Chrome child still alive 21 minutes later; the smoke waited | Once (14 Nov, about 07:43), not reproduced in later runs | Not found. `close()` had not finished: the profile folder was still there and Chrome alive, yet the PASS line, which comes after `close()`, was printed. A lingering handle or a Chrome child that would not die is the likely kind of cause. The lock itself is not held by it (close gives it back), but the smoke is stuck. | **Mitigated, not explained.** `close()` arms a 5-minute net (`armExitNet`): if the process is still running then, it says so and ends with the exit code it already had, or 1 when none was set (a process in the middle of something fails loudly). The next `launch()` disarms it. Four checks in `tests/locktest.mjs`. If you see it again, note which test and whether Chrome was alive: that is the evidence the cause needs. |
| Any Chrome test | `Chrome did not start` | 4 logs | Starting Chrome under load. | Already retried once in `cdp.mjs`; nothing more is needed. |

## Failures that are not flakes

- `no console errors (station)  exception: TypeError: Cannot read properties of null` (2 logs): the ship-shadow crash after buying an upgrade, a real bug, fixed in `space-game-interior-fix`.
- `content/...`, `contentboot`, `contentproof`, `map: shows "950 CR" in gold` and similar (several logs): branches whose content or text change was not finished or not sealed (`content.generated.js` stale). They failed alone as well.
- A script that wants a browser build the Mac does not have: a Playwright from another project asks for a headless shell that is not installed (not Long Haul's own tests, which drive Chrome over DevTools directly).
- The full smoke takes hours when many agents run it. Agents run only the suites their card touched; the batch gate runs the one full smoke (`docs/AGENTS.md`).
