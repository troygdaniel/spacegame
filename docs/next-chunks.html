Nothing done to the groups does. They come to the cruiser's turrets and die there, so when and how many matters little. Each row is the same bots on a scratch copy with one line of 2.2's table changed, six seeds; the repo was not touched.

So today nothing is at stake when the last guard runs. Chunk 6 makes that the fighter's job; chunk 7 is the only thing here that buys real time, and it buys it with waiting.

My view, for Troy to decide: 2.2 at about 55 s sits with its level (2.1 is 43 s, 2.3 is 79 s). Stop making it longer and make its one stake real.

Questions 2 to 4, in short

  1. 1.2. Leave the mission. It is the one place where taking a pilot out of the wing has a price, and the game now says so once. If the price is wanted smaller, chunk 10 is the lever.
  2. 1.3. A nag, as the bot flies it: about 8 hand-backs a run, 25 s a stay. A player is not forced to hand back, but has nowhere else to take a hurt fighter. Chunk 8 gives a place: beside the cruiser.
  3. 1.1, rules 3 and 5. Change the rules, not the mission. Doing nothing in a fighter should cost a little (chunk 11), and last hits are the wrong count when turrets finish what the fighter started (chunk 12).
