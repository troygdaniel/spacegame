3D from above: your cruiser and her fighters

Mock-ups for the Campaign. The camera stays straight above. The ships are still flat drawings, but they are lit, they roll when they turn, they cast a soft shadow, and their engines and flaps move. Nothing here is a 3D model: every picture is canvas shapes, redrawn each frame. Pick one fighter and one cruiser by letter.

How the effect is made

  • Light and shade. Each hull part is filled with a band of light across its width: a highlight, the metal's own tone, a dark edge.
  • Bank and tilt. In a turn the hull is drawn narrower (it rolls), the highlight slides to the raised side, the low side goes dark, and the raised parts (canopy, bridge, fins) shift sideways over the hull.
  • A soft shadow. The ship's outline, blurred and dark, a little down and to the right. It moves further away as a fighter climbs off the deck.
  • Moving parts. The engine flare grows with thrust and flickers; the exhaust trails and bends away from a turn; the flaps on the wings move with the stick.
  • Where the shadow shows. Space is black, so the shadow is seen over what is behind the ship: a cloud, a planet, a station, and above all the cruiser's own deck under a fighter.

Colour rules kept: your ships are pale blue metal with your blue engine glow, so they still read as yours. No pink, amber or gold is used on them.
