Long Haul around Earth

The regular game with the Campaign's look and the Campaign fighter's feel, flown around Earth. It is behind one switch, the address ?longhaul=new. Without the switch Long Haul is what it was: no file it reads is changed except where a line asks the switch first.

Three screens, drawn

Mock-ups made for this page: the shapes and colours are the Campaign's own tables (the flat-cut ships, the flat discs, the page HUD). Nothing here is a screenshot.

1. Leaving Keel Station: Earth fills the lower left behind everything, the Moon is a place you can fly to, the HUD is the Campaign's panels.

2. A fight in Debris Field One: hostiles pink, your fire mint, the thin mint ring on the lead point, a pink pill at the edge for the ones off the screen.

3. Coming up on the Moon with a call's freighter in tow: the ship you protect is amber, the goal is gold, the reach ring is blue.

The design in one paragraph

Long Haul keeps its own scene, physics, missions, money, upgrades and saves. A small bridge (src/lhbridge.js) shows the scene to the Campaign's code as if it were a mission with one flown fighter and some hostiles, so the Campaign's own functions do the work: the auto-target and its lead, the mint ring, the kill effect, the engine trails, the flat-cut pictures, the flat discs, and the page HUD's panels. Nothing of the Campaign is copied, and none of its files changes what it does.

    What is not in this pull request

      Costs and risks: every new line asks the switch before it does anything, and the Node suites that test Long Haul run with it off. The bridge reads Long Haul's sprites every frame and writes nothing back except where a piece says so (a shot's angle, a pirate's shot). Every drawing was made for this page; nothing is copied from another game, film or show.
