Every key, rule and number stays; only pictures and words move. Colour keeps its meaning (yours pale blue, a ship you protect amber, hostile pink, aliens violet, gold for goals). The two proposals are drawn from the same moment as the picture: level 1 at 95 seconds, the transport 499 km off.
