## In one minute

A browser game, plain JavaScript on Phaser 3, no build step. Two modes on one title screen:

- **The Campaign** (where all current work is): a Homeworld-style *commander* game. You fly a big, slow carrier ("the cruiser") and command its small ships (three named pilots, Wedge, Kara and Apollo) with a few keys (who, the target, the order), one short list for the cruiser, a command strip and your voice. Missions are scripted like X-Wing's (a mission is a sequence of beats with objectives and bonuses); the ship's power is not managed any more (weapons, shields and engines run at the balanced values: card `space-game-power-removed`). Earth's fleet against the aliens: three levels, nine missions, a story about an outpost, not an invasion.
- **Long Haul** (finished, not being changed): an Elite-flavoured trader and rescue game. It shares the engine, the art and the sounds.

The owner is Troy. He plays the build himself and gives feedback in chat; his feedback becomes the work. His standing goal for the Campaign (21 Nov 2026): *fights were not interactive and escorts were boring; he wants to direct the fleet more.* Feature freeze since 20 Nov: **bug fixes and the fleet-command batch only; new ideas go on the bench** (`ROADMAP.md`).

Between missions you are in **the ready room**: four rows (Missions, Refit, Rank, Launch). Enter opens a row as an overlay (missions with their briefing, the refit with what each choice changes in numbers, the rank ladder).
